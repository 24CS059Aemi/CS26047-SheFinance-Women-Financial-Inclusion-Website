# Problem Statement
In India, a significant gender gap exists in financial literacy and independence. Many women, especially from rural or semi-urban areas, lack access to personalized financial planning, understanding of government schemes, and secure investment knowledge.
**SheFinance** aims to bridge this gap by providing an intuitive, secure, and AI-driven web platform dedicated entirely to women's financial inclusion. It will offer automated budgeting, AI chatbot assistance, and a curated list of government schemes to empower women to take control of their finances.
