# Problem Statement: SheFinance

## 1. Context & Background
In India, a significant gender gap exists in financial literacy and independence. Despite increasing participation in the workforce, many women, especially from rural or semi-urban areas, lack access to personalized financial planning, understanding of government welfare schemes, and secure investment options. Traditional financial platforms are often complex, intimidating, and not tailored to the unique financial journeys of women.

---

## 2. The Core Problem
1. **Lack of Financial Literacy:** There is a severe shortage of simple, engaging, and accessible financial education resources customized for female learners.
2. **Information Fragmentation:** Information about government welfare schemes, savings policies, and investment tools is scattered across multiple complex government websites, making it difficult for women to find and apply.
3. **No Centralized Budgeting Tools:** Most household financial management is done manually or using complicated accounting software, leading to a lack of structured tracking of expenses and savings.
4. **Lack of Personalized Financial Guidance:** Personalized financial advisors are expensive and inaccessible to most everyday women, homemakers, or small entrepreneurs.

---

## 3. The Proposed Solution (SheFinance)
**SheFinance** aims to bridge this gap by providing an intuitive, secure, and AI-driven web platform dedicated entirely to women's financial inclusion. 

The website offers a unified dashboard containing:
*   **Income & Expense Tracking:** Easy budgeting tools to monitor household and individual finances.
*   **Unified Schemes Explorer:** A curated, easy-to-understand directory of government welfare schemes.
*   **AI Financial Chatbot:** Instant guidance on basic finance, budgeting, and investment strategies.
*   **Interactive Financial Education:** A curated repository of articles and videos to build long-term financial literacy.

By combining financial management tools with AI-driven education, SheFinance empowers women to take complete, independent control of their financial future.
