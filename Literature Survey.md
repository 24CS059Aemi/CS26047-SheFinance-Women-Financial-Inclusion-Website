# Literature Survey
1. **Existing Financial Apps (Groww, Zerodha, Paytm):** While these apps provide excellent investment tools, they are generalized and lack personalized education or onboarding tailored specifically for women or beginners.
2. **Government Portals (MyScheme, etc.):** These portals contain vast information but are often difficult to navigate for non-technical users.
3. **Proposed Solution:** SheFinance combines the simplicity of automated budget tracking with the specific focus of empowering women. By integrating an AI Chatbot and a unified dashboard for tracking schemes and savings, it lowers the barrier to entry for women seeking financial independence.
